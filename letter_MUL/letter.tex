\subject{Letter of Support for Dr. Yiwei Sun}

Dear Prof. Cramer,

I am writing you with respect to an application of \textbf{Dr. Yiwei Sun} for a Faculty Position in Experimental Physical at the Institute of Chemistry \& Chemical Engineering at EPFL.

I know Yiwei since 2013 when I have started collaborating with his supervisor at that time, Prof. David Dunstan, on \textbf{mechanics of carbon nanostructures}. The scope of this collaboration was to provide \textit{ab initio} support for their \textbf{high pressure Raman spectroscopy} experiments. Since then, we have kept a vivid scientific exchange (also now, when Yiwei is employed as a researcher in the company Paragraf) during which Yiwei repeatedly visited my research group ``Computational Materials Science'' here in Leoben, Austria.

To my knowledge, Yiwei's PhD project was primarily focused on carrying out the experimental studies. However, for their interpretation, a \textbf{high level of atomistic and chemical insight} into the carbon-based materials was required. To my experience, Yiwei is excelling in this area. He has developed semi-classical models of the atomistic response of these nanomaterials to high pressure. Yiwei has also participated in planning and evaluation of DFT calculations (most of the interpretation he did on his own, which to me suggests a very decent insight). He was around when I was running the DFT calculations for him, and I gave him a ``crash course'' into first principles modelling, Later, he interpreted further DFT calculations on vibrational response to externally applied pressure on his own, which he successfully used for developing a classical continuum model for interpreting his high-pressure Raman measurements. In our latest joint research work (during Yiwei's postdocotral stay in the group of Prof. Sir Colin Humphreys at the Queen Mary University of London, UK), we have moved towards studying \textbf{electronic structure} of graphene and how it is impacted by an interaction with a substrate. Our collaboration has resulted into overall six peer-reviewed publications (including four in Physical Review B).

Yiwei's major strength is \textbf{deep understanding of the carbon-based materials} together with \textbf{hands-on experience on experimental raman spectroscopy and high-pressure physics techniques}. Yiwei is a very \textbf{modest person} with a \textbf{huge self-drive} towards the set goals of his work. He works hard and, in my experience, very efficiently. He speaks fluently English.

All in all, I would like to express my \textbf{strongest recommendation} for Yiwei. I am confident he will manage to meet the requirements and expectations of the position at EPFL.

Sincerely yours,\bigskip\bigskip
